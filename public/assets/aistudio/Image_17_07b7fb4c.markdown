## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-authority digital presence situated at the intersection of judicial rigor, computer forensics, and systems engineering. The design language expresses forensic precision, cryptographically validated integrity, and institutional prestige.

### Aesthetic Foundation
The platform implements a **Technical Institutionalism** style:
- **Atmosphere:** Dark, controlled, and deeply focused. It avoids playful or hyper-casual tech trends in favor of structured data displays, analytical grids, and tactical clarity.
- **Precision Detailing:** Micro-borders with cold slate undertones, illuminated emerald indicators signaling active system verification, and high-legibility typographic hierarchies.
- **Audience Resonance:** Engineered for judges, corporate legal teams, law enforcement, enterprise compliance officers, and forensic investigators. Every pixel projects uncompromised authenticity, meticulous order, and forensic chain-of-custody discipline.

## Colors

The palette is tuned specifically for high-contrast data visualization, evidence parsing, and prolonged analytical review under low-light operating conditions.

### Core Architecture
- **Canvas Base (`#0B192C`):** Deep oceanic navy serving as the foundational viewport canvas. It anchors visual weight and eliminates screen glare.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#0F172A`):** Dark slate used for nested compartments, secondary panels, and recessed input zones.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#1E293B`):** Night blue dedicated to raised interactive cards, analytical dashboards, toolbars, and modal surfaces.
- **Structural Borders (`#334155`):** Low-luminance indigo/slate framing containers, dividers, and matrix lines to define crisp structural boundaries without distraction.

### Functional Accents
- **Active Signal / CTA (`#10B981`):** Vivid emerald green reserved for validated statuses, active system nodes, conclusive evidence markers, and high-priority administrative actions.
- **Deep Audit Badges (`#064E3B`, `#0F5132`):** Forest green grounding tones engineered for passive evidence badges, encrypted tag containers, and steady-state status pills.

### Typography & Information Layers
- **Header Pure (`#FFFFFF`):** Absolute white for primary headings, metric values, and legal case identifiers.
- **Body Pearl (`#F8FAFC`):** Low-fatigue primary body text and data labels.
- **Slate Secondary (`#94A3B8`):** Subtext, metadata, cryptographic hashes, and secondary timestamps.

## Typography

The platform utilizes **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all hierarchies without exception. Its geometric clarity combined with wide aperture contours guarantees crisp rendering of technical terminology, statutory references, and numerical evidence logs.

### Application Rules
- **Headings (Bold / ExtraBold):** Tight tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) on all headers produces an authoritative, editorial finish suitable for forensic reporting.
- **Body (Regular / Medium):** Clean, generous line-heights prevent eye strain during thorough brief reading and legal cross-referencing.
- **Labels & System Microcopy:** Micro-labels utilize upper or title casing with expanded letter-spacing (`0.02em` to `0.05em`) to evoke technical telemetry and instrumentation readouts.
- **Monospace Exclusions:** Do not introduce auxiliary sans or standard system fonts. Where raw forensic hash strings or packet dumps appear, render them using Plus Jakarta Sans with tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout operates on a disciplined 12-column analytical grid that scales fluidly down to 4 columns on mobile viewports.

### Breakpoints & Grid Composition
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, `margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Maximum content container width capped at `1440px` for optimal data density and perimeter stability.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`. Complex forensic charts reflow into stacked cards.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4 columns, `margin-mobile: 1.25rem`, `gutter-sm: 1rem`. Full width stacking for audit lists and metadata sidebars.

### Spacing Philosophy
Rhythm follows an 8-point structural system, using 4px micro-steps for tight badge padding and inline status badges. Content margins prioritize clear separation between forensic findings, technical metadata, and actionable system triggers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on prominent drop shadows. Depth is achieved via **tonal stratification** and **ambient luminescence**.

### Depth Layers
- **Ground Tier (Canvas):** `#0B192C` represents the backmost operational canvas.
- **Recessed Tier (Inset Panels / Form Controls):** `#0F172A` with a 1px continuous border (`#334155`).
- **Elevated Tier (Evidence Cards / Forensic Metrics):** `#1E293B` structured with a 1px border (`#334155`).
- **Modal & Popover Tier:** `#1E293B` elevated with an ultra-subtle ambient shadow: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(2, 6, 23, 0.7)` combined with a perimeter highlight: `inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.

### Active Glows
When selecting or activating critical nodes or cryptographic components, cards emit a directional emerald edge bleed (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #10B981, 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)`).

## Shapes

The interface balances institutional authority with modern software refinement by enforcing an exact curvature hierarchy.

### Radii Hierarchy
- **Cards, Panels & Data Containers:** Standardized at `rounded-xl` (`12px` to `16px`). This avoids the stark sterility of zero-radius brutalism while maintaining clinical precision.
- **Action Buttons & Form Controls:** Fixed at `12px` (standard `rounded-lg`) to preserve button affordance within tightly calibrated grids.
- **Verification Badges & Status Chips:** Fully pill-shaped or soft rounded-md (`6px` to `9999px` based on context), ensuring immediate differentiation from interactive cards.
